FlowMail terms of use

Last updated: 26 September 2026

FlowMail is a desktop tool for generating message workbooks from templates and spreadsheets. You are responsible for the content you enter, the source data you use, and choosing an appropriate destination for generated files.

Google Drive integration is optional. If you connect a Google account and start an upload, FlowMail places the generated workbook in the Drive folder you selected. You can disconnect your account in Settings.
